# WealthWise SA

An interactive investment portfolio builder and wealth simulator for South African investors.
Built for the Stellenbosch Business School MBA *Building Your Wealth* elective (group assignment).

**Live demo:** https://jjkoch-ops.github.io/wealthwise-sa/

## What it does

1. **Investor profile**: starting capital, monthly contribution, period, goal, income, age, inflation, fees and risk appetite.
2. **Account type**: unit trust (discretionary), retirement annuity (RA) or tax-free savings account (TFSA), with 2026/27 tax rules, TFSA limits, RA tax refunds, Regulation 28 and the two-pot system.
3. **Funds and allocation**: real South African funds per asset class (SA equities, Nasdaq-100, gold, property, crypto, cash) with their published costs, and sliders that keep the total at 100%.
4. **Wealth projection**: 1,000-path Monte Carlo simulation with optimistic, baseline and pessimistic outcomes, inflation-adjusted values and the cost of fees.
5. **Account types**: the same plan compared across unit trust, RA and TFSA after tax.
6. **Compare strategies**: conservative, balanced, aggressive and Nasdaq-100-only portfolios.
7. **Stress test**: global crash, rand collapse, SA political shock, inflation, rate hikes, crypto collapse and strong growth.
8. **Explain my portfolio**: plain-language explanation of risk, diversification, costs and account type, with experiments to try.
9. **Goal planner**: chance of reaching a goal after tax, and the monthly contribution needed for a 50%, 80% or 90% chance.

## Running it

It is a single static `index.html` file. Open it in any modern browser, or use the GitHub Pages link above. No install, login or data upload is needed.

## Disclaimer

Educational simulator only. It does not provide financial advice as defined in the FAIS Act. Fund names are illustrations, not recommendations. Return assumptions are illustrative long-run estimates, not forecasts.
